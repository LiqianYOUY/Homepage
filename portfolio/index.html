Profile

Turning ethical AI requirements into systems people can understand and control.

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of Technology Sydney with an interdisciplinary background in human-computer interaction, interaction design, and digital product development. My research focuses on transparent, controllable, and accessible automation in smart-home health technologies.

I combine human-centred design, functional prototyping, surveys, interviews, and usability evaluation to translate ethical requirements into deployable technologies. My industry experience helps connect research concepts with practical product decisions, implementation constraints, and real-world use.
