About

Designing useful systems for real people.

I am an interaction designer with a background in UI/UX, graphic design, web development, prototyping, and multimedia production. My work combines visual clarity with practical product thinking, from early research and concept framing through high-fidelity prototypes and implementation.

I am drawn to elegant minimalism: making information easier to understand, interactions easier to use, and products more memorable without unnecessary decoration. Travel, language learning, and cross-cultural experience continue to shape how I design for different people and contexts.
