Travel bookings / Interface design / 2018

Tomato Travel Platform

I designed a desktop workspace for travel teams to manage bookings, help customers, and keep track of the trips and offers they sell.

Context
Travel operations
Role
Interface design
Format
Desktop platform
Audience
Travel teams & agents
Brief Interface Scope

01 / Brief

Keep the day’s bookings and updates in view.

Travel teams have a lot to keep up with: new bookings, customer questions, available products, and changes from suppliers. This workspace brings that information together so staff can see what needs their attention.

I kept common tasks in the side menu and used short summaries to show what had changed. Staff can check booking and product updates from the main screen before opening a record.

02 / Interface

A starting point for the workday.

A

Common tasks close by

The side menu keeps bookings, customers, account settings, and support easy to find.

B

Updates at a glance

Counts and status labels help staff spot changes and decide what to look at next.

C

Know what is available

Available trips and destination offers sit alongside booking information.

03 / What I designed

A consistent design across the team’s daily tasks.

I used the same layout patterns and visual style across the dashboard, booking details, product updates, notifications, and support screens. The platform concept also informed related travel interfaces for the web and mobile.
