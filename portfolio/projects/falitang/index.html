Falitang Legal Service Platform

I designed a public legal service that helps residents find advice and speak to a lawyer. It brings together an app, tools for lawyers, community displays, and telephone consultations.

Brief The design Launch

01 / Brief

Make it easier to ask for legal help.

Falitang was the first product I worked on from the initial structure and screen designs through to launch. Residents needed a straightforward way to ask for help, while lawyers needed to manage conversations, appointments, and documents.

I focused on a few practical questions: where do I start, who can help me, and what happens next? The app gives people clear ways to contact a lawyer and see the progress of their request.

Pick up where you left off

Appointments, conversations, and documents stay with the case when someone moves from a call to the app or speaks to a different adviser.

03 / Launch

Falitang launched in 2021.

The App Store listing dates the first version to August 2021. In December 2021, Qingpu District Government published a report from Green Qingpu about the Smart Cloud Falitang service launching in two communities.

The report describes lawyer consultations available 24 hours a day, legal education, psychological support, remote mediation, and data reporting for the community service.
